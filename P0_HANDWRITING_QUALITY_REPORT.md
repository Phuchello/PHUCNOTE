Engine audit: installed perfect_freehand2.5.2+1 defaults isComplete=false, round caps and no taper. Both live and committed paths use this same historical implementation; terminal UP adds actual changed position while preserving last accepted pressure/tilt. Therefore no completion-mode visual pop is introduced, but a trailing-tip bias remains. Pressure variation >.01 disables simulation; constant pressure uses velocity simulation. This heuristic cannot establish hardware capability, and a late pressure variation can alter the live outline. Changing completion/pressure interpretation would alter historical ink; NOT changed without explicit versioned schema authorization. Controller still clamps pressure to .1..1 and retains Flutter tilt; no real device range or sampling claim.
